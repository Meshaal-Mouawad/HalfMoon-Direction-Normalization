## Contribution assessment

The verified contribution within these eight sources is the **specific combination** of an exact signed-ray zero-one loss identity, a uniform risk limit for distant normalization origins over `L¹` binary populations, a half-annulus all-origin geometry, explicit whole-plane lower certificates, and a sharp fixed-decoder one-bit interval. General decision sufficiency, wedge separation, radial-projection geometry, invariant compression, and normalization-sensitive empirical behavior have clear precedents and should remain credited as such. This comparison supports the manuscript's scoped positioning; it is not a claim of exhaustive novelty across all mathematical or machine-learning literature.
