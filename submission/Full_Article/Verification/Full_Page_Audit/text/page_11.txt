The functions Ac (θ) and Bc (θ) are signed decision-mass densities per unit angle,
not probabilities on individual zero-width rays. Their minimum describes opposing
preference cancelled at each angle; only integration across angles gives the excess Bayes
risk ∆(c), a probability. Positive loss therefore requires conflicting preferences on a set
of directions of positive angular measure. This specializes decision-preservation theory
[5, 6] to a moving normalization origin; Supplementary Section S1 derives the identity.
