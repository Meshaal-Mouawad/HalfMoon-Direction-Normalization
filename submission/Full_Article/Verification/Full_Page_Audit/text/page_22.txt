   Four representations clarify the comparison. Raw coordinates R and midpoint
direction plus continuous radius PM retain full positional information, establishing two
information-complete controls. Midpoint direction alone DM removes radius and has a

The 80 new candidate fits yield 40 selected models whose final tests are evaluated after
model selection is frozen.
   The study treats the paired B − DM and B − PM differences as their own two-
comparison family, with simultaneous 95% Bonferroni-adjusted intervals. The direction-
plus-bit and direction-plus-radius networks use three input features and 321 parameters;
the raw and direction-only controls use two features and 257 parameters. These details
matter because the evidence combines a change in the information supplied with a
documented change in input dimensionality.
