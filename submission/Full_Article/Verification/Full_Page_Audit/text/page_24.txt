7.3    Minimality and robustness are distinct results

One additional radial bit is sufficient in the midpoint geometry because the constructed
classifier uses it to choose between two valid angular decision rules. It is minimal
among finite-valued radial codes appended to exact direction because a zero-bit addition
leaves the direction-only Bayes floor unchanged. The bit does not identify the label
independently of direction; under the equal-prior reflected sampling laws its own Bayes
error is 50%. Its value is relational: it supplies the small amount of radial information
needed to choose which angular separator applies.
