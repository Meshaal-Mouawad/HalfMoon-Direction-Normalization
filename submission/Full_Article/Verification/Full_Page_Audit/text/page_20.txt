6.2    How much does changing the origin change learned error?

The six prespecified paired origin contrasts provide an inferential check on the pattern
seen in the population-risk table. Every seed contributes a paired difference computed on
the same held-out observations under two transformations. The simultaneous confidence
intervals therefore summarize across the ten independent seed-level repetitions rather
than treating hundreds of thousands of test observations as independent replicates for
the purpose of inference.
