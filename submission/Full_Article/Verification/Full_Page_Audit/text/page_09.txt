    These controls are designed around an information hierarchy. Raw coordinates and
exact direction-plus-radius inputs have the full coordinate information. Direction-only
features discard the radial component. The one-bit repair condition, evaluated sepa-
rately, restores a categorical part of the radius without restoring the continuous variable.
Comparing population Bayes risks identifies information loss exactly; comparing trained
networks shows how those distinct inputs perform under a fixed fitting protocol.
