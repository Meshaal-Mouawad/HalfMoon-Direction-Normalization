7.2    Learned performance and the exact decoder

The repaired representation has zero Bayes risk in the proved geometry, and the
explicit decoder correctly labels all points in the continuous class supports. The trained
network approximates this mapping rather than receiving the decoder’s coefficients.
On the same 500,000 test observations, direction-only networks make 54,362 errors
while the networks with the one-bit feature make three. Raw-coordinate and full-radius
models make no observed errors, and the explicit analytic decoder also makes none.
