Recovering the decision with one radius bit. The all-origin loss result rules
out fixing the midpoint case by moving the origin, but it leaves open whether a small
amount of distance information can repair the representation. The proposed bit answers
only whether an observation is near or far from the fixed midpoint; it does not report
the full distance. At h = 0.5, direction alone has positive loss at every origin. At
c = (1/2, 1/4), append q = 1{∥x − c∥ > 4/5}. For q = 0, the sign of 9u1 + 10u2
