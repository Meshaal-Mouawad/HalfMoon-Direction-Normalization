# Scoped literature verification after the unusable Consensus search

**Manuscript:** Global loss bounds and one-bit recovery for direction-normalized half-moons  
**Author:** Meshaal Mouawad  
**Date:** 19 November 2026  
**Purpose:** Re-evaluate the failed surname-based Consensus retrieval against eight named scholarly predecessors. This is an evidence-led literature check, **not an external mathematical peer review or universal novelty certificate**. No manuscript, numerical data, proof or journal submission is modified by this report.

## Audit disposition

**Consensus retrieval verdict: INVALID FOR NOVELTY.** The prior search returned ten papers in graph reconfiguration, algorithmic complexity, cough classification and monocular vision, largely sharing the surname Mouawad. Those topics do not directly test the manuscript's claims about angular Bayes risk, origin-wide risk floors, projection limits or one-bit radial repair. Crucially, the report did not retrieve the eight requested pivotal works, which independently exist and are bibliographically verifiable.

The statement "no directly relevant work found" is a description of an irrelevant retrieval result, not evidence that the proposed results are novel. This previous report must be excluded from the manuscript's scientific novelty assessment.

## Editorial consequences

The current 31-page manuscript already cites all eight pivotal sources and develops the key comparisons in Related Work and Discussion. **No additional unrelated surname-matched papers should be added.** No change to the protected mathematics, numerical findings, tables, figures or author declarations follows from the failed Consensus retrieval.

Two scholarly distinctions should continue to control wording:
1. Statistical excess risk and lossless feature transformation are established foundations; the contribution is the **specific selectable-origin geometry, global uniform projection-limit analysis and bounded one-bit repair**.
2. **Bayes-risk uniform convergence is not established by Orponen's radial-projection dimension/smoothness paper**; it is a result proposed and proved in this manuscript and needs proof-level evaluation.

## Verification scope

Primary publication titles, authors, years, DOIs and substantive topics were checked directly through journal/conference publication pages or accessible full text. The selected comparison covers the eight specified pivotal references, not a systematically exhaustive novelty review of all published mathematics or machine-learning literature. The Consensus result was excluded as irrelevant. No external expert has endorsed the proof validity, the certified global bound, or the exact optimality of the bit; those claims remain subject to independent peer review.

**Decision:** Prior Consensus report REJECTED as a novelty audit. Pivotal-source verification COMPLETE. Universal novelty and independent proof validity NOT VERIFIED. Full-length manuscript scientific record and existing PRL submission remain unchanged.
