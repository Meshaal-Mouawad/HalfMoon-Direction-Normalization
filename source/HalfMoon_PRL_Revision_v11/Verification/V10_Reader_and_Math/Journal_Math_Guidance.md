Checked: 15 November 2026. Section 2.1, PDF pages 4-5, requests a self-contained abstract of at most 200 words and excludes elaborate equations. Section 3.3, PDF page 6, specifies italic variables, right-margin equation numbering and slash-style division in running text. The live Guide for Authors returned HTTP 403; its preserved 8 November capture also requests a stand-alone abstract.

Editorial application: mathematical symbols are not prohibited from abstracts as a class. V10 removes the unexplained displacement variable and bound because they interrupt comprehension before the geometry is defined. The exact hypothesis is retained in Theorem 1 and its stronger global consequence. The former unnumbered pair of signed-ray-mass definitions now has one equation number and an explicit cross-reference. All formal displayed expressions are unchanged. Supplementary proof sources are protected and retain their established separate-document numbering.
