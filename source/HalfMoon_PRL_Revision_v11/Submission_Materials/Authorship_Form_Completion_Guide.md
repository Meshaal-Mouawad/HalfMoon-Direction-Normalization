# Official authorship form completion guide

Use the [official Pattern Recognition Letters authorship and formatting PDF](https://legacyfileshare.elsevier.com/promis_misc/patrec-authorship-and-formatting.pdf), linked by the current [Guide for Authors](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/pattern-recognition-letters/publish/guide-for-authors). The authorship confirmation is on page 1 of that six-page file.

This guide is a preparation aid. The journal requires its official form for upload.

Prepare the corresponding author's name; the actual signature and date; any preprint records; relevant conference submissions or publications; and any required republication explanation and conference-paper copies. The form asks the corresponding author to attest to publication and submission history, originality and permissions, significant author contributions, and final author approval. Each statement must reflect the author's actual circumstances.

The verified current-guide capture directs upload under **Cover Letter**; the older form labels the item **Confirmation of Authorship**. Follow the current portal item type. Its formatting pages specify seven pages, an abstract at most 200 words, required highlights and an optional graphical abstract. V5 uses the CAS template explicitly linked by the verified current-guide capture, with fresh publisher-archive verification. See `../Template/Current_Template_Verification.md`.

The official PDF text was inspected on 8 November 2026. Direct file retrieval returned an HTML “Site Unavailable” page, so this package contains the verified official link and this completion aid. No unsigned reconstruction has been labeled an official form.
