## V24 revision

The article gives a criterion for exact feedback coding with measurable radial encoders. The half-annuli and a population with a separate angular sector for each conflicting pair can have the same minimum one-way alphabet of \(M\) radial symbols. With a one-bit radial reply, they require \(M-1\) and \(\lceil\log_2 M\rceil\) hints, respectively. At the evaluated displacement 0.77, the one-way minimum is 13 symbols, so the corresponding hint counts are 12 and 4. The revision also derives the total fixed-length communication costs and positions the findings in contemporary work on decision-aware compression. The author-only novelty review documents these revisions.

The supplement uses the V20 material and carries the revised V24 article title. Supplementary Section S17 and Table S7 give the numerical width checks for Corollary 4. The [versioned V18 supporting dataset](https://github.com/Meshaal-Mouawad/HalfMoon-Direction-Normalization/tree/bb7a3992642fe1f88b185642858d3ed3ffe8e478/research/radial-coding-v18) contains the coding calculations and supporting data. The V11 dataset supplies the original neural runs and oracle records.
